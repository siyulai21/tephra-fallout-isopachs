# New eruption intake

[Workflow](../../docs/NEW_ERUPTION.md) · [CSV definitions](../../data/README.md)

- [INTAKE.md](INTAKE.md): source, units, location and deposit notes to fill in when known.
- [observations.csv](observations.csv): header only, with no invented example measurements.

Copy these into a working intake or supply the information in chat. Do not add empty templates to the accepted dataset catalogue. At least one consistent source-coordinate pair and positive thicknesses or documented positive bounds are required for an analysis.
