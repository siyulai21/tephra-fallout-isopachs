# Eruption results

Choose an eruption for its accepted observations, four volume estimates, maps and local support audit. All volumes measure bulk airborne fallout. The two regional fits cover the observation hull. The two global fits extend across unsampled ground.

| Eruption | Accepted thickness evidence | What to keep in mind |
|---|---|---|
| [Aso-4](aso-4/README.md) | 83 exact, 3 lower bounds, 1 upper bound | Reviewed cruise positions and bounds. Marine sampling leaves the source region and several bearings poorly constrained. |
| [B-Tm](b-tm/README.md) | 64 measurements, 3 lower bounds, 1 upper bound | Combined fall units from both phases. The [source audit](../data/audits/B-Tm/README.md) distinguishes measured beds, count-only records and site maxima. |
| [Mazama](mazama/README.md) | 45 exact | The published calculation uses the supplied acceptance flags. |
| [Minoan](minoan/README.md) | 92 exact, 9 lower bounds | All accepted Plinian and co-ignimbrite fallout fans form one deposit. |
| [Tambora](tambora/README.md) | 26 exact | Four of six full-data directional fits carry a numerical warning. |
| [Younger Toba Tuff (YTT)](ytt/README.md) | 46 exact | The audited principal selection includes two qualified Maninjau beds and the U1445B core. |
