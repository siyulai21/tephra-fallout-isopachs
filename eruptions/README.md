The regional peers share the same input, geographic holdouts and integration footprint. Global mass is a separate conditional estimate. Support failures remain visible and do not veto that estimate. These are not interchangeable volume definitions.

## What lives in a case?

`README.md` is the maintained case summary. `results/` contains the reviewed regional report, maps, candidate tables and provenance; its `support/` and `global/` folders answer the other two questions. Accepted measurements and source decisions have a single authoritative home under [data/](../data/README.md).

A case can link targeted studies without promoting every experiment to the current model. Historical results are available through [Git history](../docs/HISTORY.md); required reproduction references remain with the study that uses them.
