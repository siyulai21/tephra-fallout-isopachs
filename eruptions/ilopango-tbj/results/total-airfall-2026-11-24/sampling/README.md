# Sampling evidence

The 89 directional and spherical attempt records resolve to 77 distinct posterior files. The table maps each fit and attempt to its exact bytes, including rejected attempts and identical draws reused by more than one report. The raw sampler flag records each attempt. The family reports give the final assessment of the selected sample and its integration.
