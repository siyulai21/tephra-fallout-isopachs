# Directional extrapolation in unsampled bearings

The measured-fan control tests three observed fan widths and four cross-bearing range ratios. Its geographic scores are reported beside the single-structure and fixed-direction controls on each current input. Mazama uses all 300 audited localities. [Current geographic scores](../fixed-ensemble/README.md) · [Mazama measured-fan fit](../../../eruptions/mazama/results/directional/bearing/README.md)

The model fits only quantitative ash evidence in the measured-fan comparison. Documented archive limits can enter a separate sensitivity with their own preservation and detection assumptions. The B-Tm study checks what happens in the western sector under those assumptions. [Archive protocol](../../../docs/methods/archive-evidence.md) · [B-Tm bearing and archive fits](../../../eruptions/b-tm/results/directional/bearing/README.md)

The calibration retains its original training observations and withheld-row identities. A target contributes no scores to its own weights. [Calibration rows, weights and posterior hashes](../../../src/empirical_isopach/assets/bearing-calibration.json)
