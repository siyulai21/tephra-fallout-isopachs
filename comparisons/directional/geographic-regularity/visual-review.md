# Direct final-field visual review

The final installed case plates were also inspected after alignment to the shared family frames. Aso-4 uses ±200 km (12 sites), B-Tm ±50 km (9), Tambora ±125 km (6), YTT ±800 km (5), and Minoan ±60 km (18). These wider case frames supplement the fixed proximal, intermediate and full paired views below. Aso-4's published 32 cm inner envelope stays rounded within the northeast/southeast family. B-Tm retains its compact, steep upwind core within the long eastern fan. Tambora's 64 cm closure and evidence-associated 16 cm thin hole remain visible at the wider frame. YTT's enlarged inset includes its nearest actual sites; it does not resolve the unobserved ±100 km source area. Minoan's widened inset shows the eastern 16 cm lobe and southwestern thin sector without changing their geometry. The final Ilopango, Mazama, Satsuma and Pinatubo case plates were inspected too. Every case's inner contours were followed into its full-deposit picture, and every case's final three-scale exceedance-probability plate was reviewed.

## Tambora

Inspected the full paired plate at all three scales. The 32/64 cm proximal closures have continuous rounded curvature; the 64 cm core shifts modestly west and south without a pointed vent. At intermediate scale the 32 cm western shoulder remains attached through a broad saddle toward the two western readings. The small 16 cm thin area east of the source is evaluated against the actual nearby thin reading; it is not an unsupported thick island. Across the full footprint the 0.5–8 cm curves form a consistent elongated northwest–southeast family, with gentler southern flattening and less repeated waviness than v5. The compact proximal family remains legible in that full context. Sparse proximal coverage (four sites inside ±100 km) leaves the source maximum and the eastern thin-area shape conditional.

## Aso-4

Inspected the direct paired plate at all scales. The new conditional 64 cm source closure is a smooth northwest–southeast oval; it contains no proximal observation, with only three accepted sites in the ±100 km frame. It must not be described as a measured vent thickness. The 32 cm envelope is rounder and continuous, while the 8–16 cm family expands into the southeast dispersal lobe. The separate northeast reach along Japan remains visible across the full deposit; the transition between lobes is a broad concavity, without an angular source cusp or detached thick patch. The full outer curves are smoother and more evenly spaced, with a broad eastern and southern continuation that remains weakly observed. A small 4 cm shoulder near (400,410) km is checked against its nearby actual reading. Following the source closures through the intermediate window and into the full map shows a compact coherent core within the two larger dispersal directions.

## B-Tm

Inspected the direct paired plate at ±50 km, intermediate range and the full eastward deposit. The proximal 32 cm shoulder retains the measured eastward saddle, but the adjacent lower contours have smoother curvature and a more consistent progression in width. The source looks compact within the full fan; its steep upwind gradient is not a plotting cusp. The intermediate high-thickness lobe closes smoothly downwind. At distal range the old repeated north–south undulations and flat eastern endings are reduced; the outer family closes in broad rounded arcs. The inner distal high region remains associated with actual thick marine readings, so it is not erased merely to make every level connected. The northwest upper-limit evidence still constrains the upwind side. Geographic and quadrant scores are slightly worse for this case despite substantially better fitted-site agreement; continuation beyond the observed eastern sites remains conditional.

B-Tm evidence anchors: rows 62–64 (Pan trenches 1, 2 and 4; 90, 50 and 50 cm) and row 61 (Yuanchi/Pan section; 72 cm) support the 64 cm downwind lobe. The adjacent 20 cm Pan trench 5 (row 65) and 16 cm Dongfanghong reading (row 3) support its northern shoulder. The plotted inner brown line entering the proximal frame is the separate 64 cm level, not an incision in the 32 cm line. Distal rows 4, 5, 8–10 retain 9–11 cm marine readings. Quanyang peat (row 71; 0.64 cm upper limit) constrains the northwest flank.

Tambora evidence anchor: row 4 at (79.3,-22.2) km reports 10 cm, supporting the enclosed thin region at the 16 cm level; rows 2 and 3 east of the vent report 120 and 90 cm. Aso-4 evidence anchors for the 4 cm shoulder: row 67 at (387.4,407.8) km reports 6 cm, row 68 at (465.9,394.7) km reports 4 cm and row 21 farther south reports 3.5 cm.

## YTT

Inspected the direct paired plate at all three scales. The ±100 km view contains no observations and no qualifying crossing; the 64 cm closure lies outside it. This blank close-up is retained rather than fabricating a near-vent constraint. At intermediate scale the 64 cm ring has smooth, nearly elliptical curvature and the 32 cm envelope expands northward; the former abrupt change near the Malay side is gentler. Geographic prediction worsens for YTT even though fitted-site agreement and quadrant prediction improve.

## Minoan — final 864-state paired direct maps

Inspected proximal ±40 km, intermediate ~190 km, and the full ~1,500 km-wide plate. The revised outer 0.5–4 cm family has smooth, broad eastward extension and a much gentler western waist; its centre and orientation change continuously with level. The 8 cm southwestern thin area now joins the southern thin sector rather than forming the old detached thin enclosure. Rows 6 and 37 are actual 5 cm points at (-27.9,-15.2) and (-19.0,-7.6) km. The near-vent 16 cm shoulder remains east of Santorini; rows 10 (31 cm), 7 (5 cm) and 13 (46 cm) within a few kilometres explain the local saddle. There is no forced thickness peak at the vent. In the intermediate and full views, genuinely heterogeneous marine thicknesses still produce several small thick patches and thin holes. Rows 39 and 97 (51 and 44 cm, near 21,-69 km) support a southern 16 cm patch, while rows 53 and 54 (1 and 1.3 cm, near 1,-48 and -6,-60 km) support adjacent thin areas. The eastern 32 cm patch lies by rows 73 (35 cm), 57 (212 cm) and 24 (32 cm), not in an empty direction. These short-range contrasts should not propagate as repeated ripples around all distal contours. The new full-view family separates the compact, locally complex inner field from the broad, smoother outer continuation. Remaining local features are evidence-conditioned; distant western, southern and eastern tails are conditional. Geographic score improves very slightly; quadrant score is effectively unchanged.

## Mazama — final 864-state paired direct maps

## Pinatubo Layer C — final 864-state paired direct maps

Inspected proximal ±20 km (61 sites), intermediate ~125 km (200 sites), and full deposit (228 of 235 locations in the common frame). The near-vent 8 and 16 cm contours retain the curved east–west band and the eastward saddle constrained by close, contrasting point readings. Rows 92–94 are 7, 20 and 18 cm; rows 79 and 80 farther north are 4.5 and 7 cm. The field does not force a local vent maximum. Intermediate contours keep the pronounced eastern thinning and southwestern extension, with smoother transitions between the proximal and marine sectors. Some sharp-looking small offshore departures remain because actual evidence supports them. Row 199 (H01-16, -233.4,112.8 km) is a tight 0.1–0.2 cm range at the northern 0.5 cm indentation. Row 190 (W04-SO-132-14, -369.9,-31.1 km) is 3.0–3.2 cm and supports a local 2 cm patch. Row 209 (S99-SO-114-23, -281.4,-117.1 km) is 1 cm at the southern 2 cm indentation. Row 204 (H01-25, -382.7,-141.9 km) is 0.6–0.7 cm at the local 1 cm contrast. These are quantitative constraints, not absences inferred from empty ocean. Their narrow influence now reflects a fixed physical local scale instead of distorting several far-reaching lobes. It would be misleading to call every such notch an artifact solely because it is visually irregular. All three primary scores improve, including both geographic designs.

## Cross-level numerical screen

The high-resolution screening mesh reports slightly less length-weighted absolute turning and lower high-harmonic radius variation overall. Counts of holes and disconnected regions do not uniformly decrease: fixed physical-scale local departures can represent a genuinely thin or thick reading more locally. Every v6 secondary patch flagged on the fine mesh contains a supporting point/range, or disappears in the corresponding direct local check. Every v6 thin-hole flag likewise contains thin evidence or fails to persist in the flagged region on both direct local resolutions. The diagnostics are screens, not proofs that every unsampled contour segment is geological truth. In particular, a range overlapping a threshold is weaker support than a range wholly above or below it. The retained feature table distinguishes enclosed row IDs, supporting row IDs and direct checks; the case-by-case notes give the consequential examples.
