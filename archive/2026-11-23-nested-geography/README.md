# Nested geography before the dispersal-footprint revision

The eight unchanged inputs retain their preceding corridor map, integral and thickness–area table as controls. The paired Mazama controls below use all 300 current localities. Earlier reader pages and maps are available at their original revision. [Eight-eruption comparison](cases.csv) · [Original nine-eruption publication](https://github.com/siyulai21/tephra-fallout-isopachs/tree/332d673b290eecacefa646fbc4f7ee238ce73495)

The current Mazama comparison uses 300 localities for both preceding geographies and the fitted footprints. Their matched controls isolate the effect of geography. [300-locality corridor integral](mazama-300-controls/corridor/deposit.json) · [300-locality whole-area integral](mazama-300-controls/whole/median-field.json)

The first replacement trial used moving ellipses with a fixed 0.8 secondary scale, constant axis ratio near the source and a union of the two source-connected ellipses. It removed the station-aligned ridges but carried Mazama's secondary lobe far beyond the evidence and admitted a four-pronged proximal shape. The final model uses a common proximal deposit and infers secondary persistence. The trial's ten sets of full and held-out fit receipts and their input identities are retained.
