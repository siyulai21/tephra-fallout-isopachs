# Nested geography before the dispersal-footprint revision

Each eruption directory here retains the preceding median-map image, map integral and thickness–area table. The original corridor draws, per-site predictions and finite-spread calculations remain under the corresponding eruption's historical nested result. Previous case pages and catalogue figures are collected with their path hashes. [Original repository revision](https://github.com/siyulai21/tephra-fallout-isopachs/tree/332d673b290eecacefa646fbc4f7ee238ce73495) · [Previous comparison](cases.csv) · [Previous pages and figures](previous-case-pages.zip) · [Checksums](previous-case-pages.sha256.json)

The published Mazama comparator used 271 localities. A separate refit of both preceding geographies to all 300 current localities identifies the effect of geography on an identical likelihood. [300-locality corridor integral](mazama-300-controls/corridor/deposit.json) · [300-locality whole-area integral](mazama-300-controls/whole/median-field.json)

The first replacement trial used moving ellipses with a fixed 0.8 secondary scale, constant axis ratio near the source and a union of the two source-connected ellipses. It removed the station-aligned ridges but carried Mazama's secondary lobe far beyond the evidence and admitted a four-pronged proximal shape. The final model uses a common proximal deposit and infers secondary persistence. The trial's ten sets of full and held-out fit receipts and their input identities are retained. [Mazama trial map](fixed-secondary/mazama.png) · [Pinatubo trial map](fixed-secondary/pinatubo-1991-layer-c.png) · [Trial posteriors' identities and predictions](fixed-secondary/fit-receipts.zip) · [Geographic source](fixed-secondary/geometry.py.txt) · [Inference source](fixed-secondary/posterior.py.txt)

To reconstruct the trial in a separate checkout of this release, substitute the two archived source files for their counterparts under the nested footprint package before fitting.
