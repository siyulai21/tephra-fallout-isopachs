# Directional model development

This archive records the investigation preceding the [geographic regularity study](../../comparisons/directional/geographic-regularity/README.md). The matched starting point is revision [beee82b](https://github.com/siyulai21/tephra-fallout-isopachs/tree/beee82b7020d62aa2c3dc847d74e45dcf651084f). Every accepted observation, reported minimum, upper limit and range remained unchanged. No eruption-specific parameter or prescribed volume was used.

## What was tested

The [exploratory ledger](all-attempts.csv) and [later partition ledger](qualified-partitions.csv) retain successes and failures. `fit-attempts.tar.gz` contains the exploratory receipts and site scores; `qualification-attempts.tar.gz` contains the subsequent receipts, attempted numerical resolutions, scores, survey transfers and synthetic checks. The accompanying text logs retain numerical warnings, interrupted development jobs and resource failures. These were development comparisons: geographic scores, synthetic outcomes and visual reviews informed the decisions. They are not independent confirmatory tests or a preregistered search.

The exploration included changed radial harmonics, transport and spatial covariances, physical-distance and diffusive local kernels, full and grouped spatial bases, joint curvature penalties, heavier-tailed residuals, coherent-fraction changes and survey-reach ablations. Several improved one scalar score but produced unsupported detached regions or unnatural shared shoulders. Smoother Gaussian broad kernels, for example, created unsupported Mazama and Minoan patches; their paired figures are retained here. Reducing the fine coherent fraction to a quarter increased an unsupported Mazama patch. Neither was promoted. A broad/fine separation with a fixed physical fine scale addressed the source of those artifacts while preserving stronger local agreement.

The untapered separated model completed all estimable full, geographic and quadrant fits, but had broad tails and several unresolved volume refinements. The tempered model inferred its exponential cutoff from the thickness likelihood, independently of survey reach. A separate extension admitted pure exponential means. It added complexity with little average geographic benefit and weaker quadrant prediction. The selected family retains the power-law and tempered power-law means.

Another extension inferred a quarter, half or full spatial amplitude. It recovered the smooth synthetic deposit more closely, but weakened geographic prediction and measured-site agreement in Minoan and Satsuma. Its conditional intervals also missed the larger invisible-fan alternatives. It was rejected. Separate broad-amplitude adaptation did not resolve the tradeoff. The final model keeps the preceding 1.15 log-unit amplitude; it does not tune amplitude or total volume to individual eruptions or synthetic truths. The interrupted remainder of the rejected adaptive-amplitude run was not used as a successful qualification.

## Reproducibility and numerical failures

`prototype-engine/` preserves the exploratory implementation; `untapered-v6-engine/`, `tempered-power-engine/`, `exponential-fixed-amplitude-engine/` and `adaptive-amplitude-engine/` preserve later source snapshots. The early exploration script evolved during the investigation, so the early ledger is a development record, not a claim that every historical row can be regenerated from the final prototype alone. Fully identified later partitions retain their engine, observations, posterior and scores hashes. Current publication uses the separately signed final engine and matched observations.

The first broad-amplitude probe accidentally used inconsistent feature scaling in its predictor. The independent CDF check detected the error, with a maximum discrepancy of about 0.42. Its failed receipts remain under `broad-amplitude-probe-inconsistent-display` in the archive. The corrected probe passed that check but did not justify promotion. This failure illustrates why the published field is checked against an independent conditional calculation.

Whole-field integration now refines parameter populations separately from already converged spatial rules, retaining the original acceptance tolerances. Bound integration operates in the constrained latent subspace with analytic conditional control variates. These changes improved numerical convergence without changing an upper limit or range into a point reading. Historical v5 packets retain their original covariance, source gate and attenuation through explicit versioned replay.

The exploratory feature tables use interpolated integration grids and are screening diagnostics. The final study evaluates displayed contours directly from the posterior and revisits suspect features on finer local grids. Only that final review supports the published geographical conclusions.

## Publication checks

`publication-checks/` retains the final test and replay logs together with the earlier publication failures. The final qualification record identifies the successful reruns. The initial checksum failures were resolved by identifying reviewed replacements and checking nine already-stale inventory entries against their unchanged bytes at the base revision. No accepted source table changed.

The catalogue now declares B-Tm's four actual local packets and preserves the separately linked external spherical preview. The registry no longer advertises aggregate `release.json` files absent from five current case directories; the retained family packets and the two existing complete-release receipts are verified independently. Historical input receipts are preserved. The uncertain-azimuth control table reads its own reports and declares their actual input editions, instead of borrowing principal map receipts from another model or edition. The spherical paired summary now enumerates the eight locally available current packets. These publication corrections do not change another family's fitted posterior.
