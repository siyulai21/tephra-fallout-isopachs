# Development visual inspection, before final release

All nine baseline three-scale plates were viewed. The candidate findings below refer to unsmoothed development grids, not yet to the final released maps. Final plates must be read from the same median grid used for the volume. These observations are diagnostics, not targets or site-specific parameter choices.

- Mazama: the tiny circular 512 cm summit disappears and the 256 cm region connects geometrically to the northeast and southeast lobes. The two lobes remain distinct in curvature and spacing. Some additional southeast thin-contour reach is conditional, while the main northeast reach shortens. Measured median absolute log error improves; interval-median violations do not all improve.
- Tambora: the near-source field remains fairly round on sparse observations. The outer axis turns gradually northwest rather than retaining concentric ellipses. Do not force fine lobes unsupported by these 26 records. Independent full-map populations required refinement.
- Aso-4: broad unseen western and southern sectors remain a material uncertainty. The revised intermediate/outer axes change gently; most proximal outlines remain rounded. Geometry should not be judged identified from numerical convergence alone.
- B-Tm: the upwind near-source thickness contracts strongly, and the formerly rigid fan can rotate gradually. The Quanyang constraint materially changes the northwest side. The full fan broadens in the southeast where not fully surveyed. Refinement to 12,288 particles per population resolves an initial independent-map disagreement.
- YTT: the unsupported northern 1 cm extension shortens from roughly 9,000 to 5,000 km in the development plate. The outer southward limb extends farther. The 16 cm region has a coherent noncircular northwest/southeast axis in the intermediate and full views. The proximal panel has no contour: no observations identify a local summit there, and no peak is invented.
- Minoan: the proximal 16 cm contour tilts southeast and transitions continuously to an eastward outer lobe. Thin contours are narrower across wind but can extend farther along wind than before. The elongated empty eastern continuation remains conditional.
- Ilopango: asymmetric proximal ellipses replace the rigid circular bullseye, with coherent scale transitions. However, the northwest 1 cm reach increases: the maximum within the NW 45-degree sector is 651 km versus 421 km previously; southeast reaches are 776 versus 828 km. Round-core, centered-core, far-focus, and focus-prior trials did not establish an improvement. No absent sites or prescribed sector truncation were supplied to force a smaller outline.
- Satsuma: the southern 1 cm reach shortens from about 500 to 350 km, with some increased northwestern reach. The collective field turns from the near-source thicker region into an elongated southeast outer field. The small 64 cm near region remains approximately rounded; west/east thickness variation is not fully explained. Keep the broad alternative and source uncertainty.
- Pinatubo: the abrupt western/southern junction is replaced by a continuous curved transition. The near 16 cm contour contracts on the eastern side and connects to the southwest structure. The unsampled far-south limb shortens; the westward 1 cm limb remains extended among marine ranges. Point RMSE improves about 14%, upper-median violations decline 11 to 5, but interval-median violations increase 13 to 18. Interval event probabilities improve overall. Raw tail-volume population warnings persist in development, and the final packet must retain them.

All contours in these plates belong to a single posterior field. No line smoothing, contour-specific fitting, volume-target matching, or eruption-specific prior has been used.
