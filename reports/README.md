## Scope and history

An observation hull can include large gaps and omit proximal deposits. These are regional bulk fallout volumes, not total eruption volumes or DRE. Conditional intervals omit parameter/selection uncertainty; reports also show geographic-deletion sensitivity and withheld coverage.

`comparison/` is the current two-method result. `global/` and `regional/` retain earlier GP-only results with their original numerical provenance. YTT's [archive](ytt/archive/README.md) retains superseded input selections. The [earlier global comparison](global-fallout/README.md) and [earlier rectangular comparison](regional-comparison/README.md) are separate historical analyses.

Minoan still combines all accepted Plinian/co-ignimbrite fallout fans in one field; identified turbidites remain excluded. Accepted input bytes and audit decisions are unchanged.
