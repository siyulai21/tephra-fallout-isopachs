# Multiscale directional isopachs

The directional ensemble estimates a continuous positive thickness field for an eruption. Unknown fan direction is integrated over the posterior. The map uses the marginal median thickness at each location. Its integral over the finite WGS84 Earth gives the principal bulk fallout volume in km³. The same prior, spatial scales, likelihood and numerical checks apply to every eruption. [Case maps and matched tests](../../comparisons/directional/multiscale/README.md)

## What determines a contour

The previous direction model joined local estimates on planar triangles and transitioned to a bearing-dependent extrapolation. A change of triangle could change the slope at a station. A distal station could select a long range for a narrow set of bearings. These effects could repeat at several thickness levels or make an angular corner through a row of proximal stations. The current fit constructs every contour from one field. It permits a thick downwind patch, a changing dispersal axis, several lobes and enclosed holes. It imposes no vent maximum, convexity or monotonicity on individual rays. [Previous model](uncertain-azimuth.md) · [Before and after maps](../../comparisons/directional/multiscale/README.md)

Let $r$ be vent-centred radial distance on the equal-area disk in km, $\theta$ the bearing, $R$ the authalic Earth radius and $x=r/L$. The likelihood and field use natural logarithms of thickness in cm. In one ensemble member the broad mean is

$$
m(r,\theta)=\log 100-p\log(1+\sqrt{x^2+0.12^2}-0.12)-s\log(1+r^4/H(\theta,r)).
$$

The constant is the prior location of the intercept. The fitted intercept has a 2.8 natural-log-unit prior standard deviation. Two further radial terms allow the shoulder of the deposit to expand or contract. Define $h=x/\sqrt{1+x^2}$, $u=x^2/(16+x^2)$ and $b=\sqrt{\max(0,1-(r/2R)^2)}$. The directional columns for harmonic $k$ are $h(hb)^k\cos(k\theta)$ and $h(hb)^k\sin(k\theta)$. A simpler member uses $k=1,2$. A member with changing fan direction uses $k=1,2,3$ with a second coefficient pair multiplied by $0.85u$. Each pair has independent centred Gaussian coefficients with the same variance, giving uniform prior phase. Their combination can split, merge and change bearing continuously with distance. Higher-order features lose amplitude toward the vent. Bearing dependence vanishes at the antipode. The method infers deposit shape from fallout evidence and supplies no eruption-specific winds or phase labels. [Coefficients and covariance](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/geometry.py)

The fourth-power reach $H$ uses positive-evidence locations in the training set, including lower and interval bounds. In the near-vent limit it is $35^4+\sum_i w_i r_i^4[0.12^4+\cos^8((\theta-\theta_i)/2)]$. The angular terms taper by $b^k$ toward the antipode, and $H$ has a $35^4$ floor. Weights are proportional to reciprocal local sampling density and sum to one. This broad interpolation of *reach* allows a fan to extend across a gap, with increasing attenuation where positive samples give little range information. It is a survey-dependent prior. A long distal transect can still influence it disproportionately, and a fan missed by the survey can be underestimated. Upper limits act through the likelihood. A missing bearing supplies no observation of thickness. [Training-only reach calculation](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/spread.py)

## Local geology and the meaning of a site reading

A two-scale Matérn-5/2 covariance represents local departures from the broad mean. The local length increases with $\sqrt{35^2+r^2}$ in the equal-area chart. At each site it grows equally along and across the vent bearing. The shorter component has a third of the longer length and one fifth of the covariance. The nonstationary determinant factor preserves positive definiteness. Spatial differences shrink toward the antipode. The chart retains equal area while distal lengths can differ from geodesic lengths. [Nonstationary covariance construction, Paciorek and Schervish](https://doi.org/10.1002/env.785)

For each locality, its three closest correlated distinct locations, including itself, can define one local average if the weakest covariance is at least 0.20. A rank-revealing factorization retains distinct averages, up to one third of the station count. Orthogonal finer-scale contrasts use the remaining capacity in a 96-mode budget. The principal fit assigns half the resolvable contrast variance to coherent deposit variation. Unrepresented variance enters the site likelihood. This lets a group of stations support a feature across its neighborhood while allowing an isolated exposure to differ from the regional deposit. The averages define basis functions. Every likelihood term comes from a source row. Values of zero and one for the coherent fraction are published as structural sensitivities across every eruption. The data do not uniquely determine this split. [Structural sensitivity](../../comparisons/directional/multiscale/resolution-sensitivity.csv)

A measured thickness and a lower-only thickness both contribute a Gaussian log-thickness density at the stated value. An upper limit contributes a cumulative probability and a range contributes the probability between its two endpoints. Field uncertainty, residual site variance and reported measurement error enter the predictive distribution separately. Only an audited quantitative limit enters the fit for a non-detection. Qualitative observations have predicted thicknesses and, where a response was specified, a separate probability calculation. The same site is counted once. Proximal deposits can be eroded or redistributed and a distal marine core can record locally unusual thickness. The model cannot identify a cause from an isolated residual. [Observation contract](sites-without-measured-thickness.md) · [Uncertainty in tephra-thickness mapping, Engwell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00445-015-0942-y)

## Inference and checks

There are 576 equally weighted combinations before fitting. The radial scale $L$ is 8, 32, 128 or 512 km, the thinning exponent $p$ is 2.75, 4.5 or 7, and the outer attenuation $s$ is 0.20 or 0.85. There are two harmonic structures, three local length multipliers (0.20, 0.50, 1.10) and four residual standard deviations (0.26, 0.48, 0.80, 1.20). These are explicit common modelling choices. All 576 log evidences and weights, including boundary fits, are saved for each partition. All geometry, priors that depend on station locations, coefficients and weights are rebuilt after deleting a region. The posterior weights condition on the specified candidate family. [Fitting algorithm](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/fit.py)

Conditioning on exact readings is analytic. For bounded observations, expectation propagation gives an importance proposal. Two independently scrambled Sobol rules correct its evidence using the original event probabilities and sample the conditional Gaussian law given bounded observations. A coarse 16-point screening rule retains candidates containing 99.999% of its estimated mass for refined integration. The final mixture retains at least 99.95% of estimated mass. A small Gaussian mixture approximates the corrected marginals. Compression is checked against the corrected distributions at the fitted locations, the vent and twelve bearings at ten radii from 4 to 12,000 km. Checks at five quantiles include a maximum 0.035 pooled CDF error, a maximum 0.06 difference between scrambles, mean relative effective sample size above 0.14, weighted evidence discrepancy below 0.10 and maximum proposal residual below 0.0001. Failed checks trigger higher sample counts and additional mixture components. Failed attempts and unresolved folds remain in the reports. [Censored inference](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/conditioning.py) · [Fitted and deleted partitions](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/validation.py)

Independent spatial integrations and mesh refinement check the median and both pointwise quartile maps. A separate calculation samples whole coherent deposits and integrates each draw over Earth. Total-volume 95% limits must agree between two spatial scrambles within 4%, between independent parameter populations within 10%, and after refinement within 8%, with a ceiling of 8,192 draws per population and 32,768 spatial points per scramble. The archive retains all attempted resolutions, sector allocations and fractions inside the connected survey or beyond observed radii. The integral of the pointwise median can differ from the median integrated draw. Likewise the integrated pointwise quartiles and the 95% interval of total draws answer different questions. The support screen checks observed brackets, unsupported holes and deletion stability independently. Each case includes a three-scale probability map for 1 cm and half its thickest displayed doubling level, with a 2 cm floor for the latter. These probabilities refer to the latent deposit. The observed-reading variance enters when predicting a site's recorded measurement or limit. [Volume integration](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/integrals.py) · [Support protocol](supported-isopachs.md)

## Reproduction

The ordinary intake runs the multiscale fit and its historical directional controls. For the directional study alone, from the repository root, use a fresh work folder and the following commands. Use the same work and release folders for the validation and plots. [New eruption intake](../NEW_ERUPTION.md)

```sh
python -m benchmarks.directional_multiscale --work results/direction/fits --jobs 3
python -m benchmarks.directional_release --work results/direction/fits --output results/direction/maps
python -m benchmarks.directional_evaluation --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study
python -m benchmarks.directional_scale_maps --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study
python -m benchmarks.directional_morphology --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study/geometry.csv.gz
python -m benchmarks.directional_predictive --work results/direction/fits --output results/direction/study/fold-marginals
python -m benchmarks.directional_resolution --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study/resolution-sensitivity.csv
python -m benchmarks.directional_challenges --output results/direction/challenges --jobs 3
python -m benchmarks.directional_experiments --challenges results/direction/challenges --output results/direction/study
python -m benchmarks.regional --output results/direction/regional
python -m benchmarks.verify_directional_multiscale
```
