# Multiscale directional isopachs

The directional ensemble estimates a continuous positive thickness field for an eruption. Unknown fan direction is integrated over the posterior. The map uses the marginal median thickness at each location. Its integral over the finite WGS84 Earth gives the principal bulk fallout volume in km³. The same prior, spatial scales, likelihood and numerical checks apply to every eruption. [Current paired maps and tests](../../comparisons/directional/geographic-regularity/README.md) · [Original multiscale study](../../comparisons/directional/multiscale/README.md)

## What determines a contour

The v6 model separates broad dispersal from local exposure-scale variability. The previous v5 model let both spatial lengths grow in proportion to distance from the vent and applied their shared-variation split to the same capped basis. Adjacent distal readings could therefore influence a very large area, displace a maximum away from the observations, or generate a shoulder repeated across several isopachs. Dense surveys could exhaust the 96-mode budget before any finer contrasts were represented. A survey-dependent reach prior also treated the arrangement of sampled directions as information about thinning. The new model addresses these mechanisms directly. No filtering, contour deletion or fitted-surface smoothing is applied after inference.

Every level is a contour of one positive field. The field permits changing bearings, multiple lobes, enclosed thin areas and secondary thick regions. No convexity, vent maximum or monotonicity along rays is imposed. The same constants and likelihood apply to every eruption; no named eruption, prescribed shape or volume target enters fitting. The visual review checks individual contours and their joint spacing, curvature, orientation, eccentricity, topology and lobe transitions against actual site evidence. [Review criteria](../ADDING_METHOD.md#visual-and-geological-review-of-isopachs)

Let $r$ be vent-centred radial distance on the equal-area disk in km, $\theta$ the bearing, $R$ the authalic Earth radius and $x=r/L$. The likelihood and field use natural logarithms of thickness in cm. In one ensemble member the broad mean is

$$
g(r)=\sqrt{x^2+0.12^2}-0.12,\qquad m(r)=\log 100-p\log(1+g(r))-s g(r).
$$

The constant is the prior location of the intercept. The fitted intercept has a 2.8 natural-log-unit prior standard deviation. Two further radial terms allow the shoulder of the deposit to expand or contract. Define $h=x/\sqrt{1+x^2}$, $u=x^2/(16+x^2)$ and $b=\sqrt{\max(0,1-(r/2R)^2)}$. The directional columns for harmonic $k$ are $h^2(hb)^k\cos(k\theta)$ and $h^2(hb)^k\sin(k\theta)$. A simpler member uses $k=1,2$. A member with changing fan direction uses $k=1,2,3$ with a second coefficient pair multiplied by $0.85u$. Each pair has independent centred Gaussian coefficients with the same variance, giving uniform prior phase. Their combination can split, merge and change bearing continuously with distance. Near the vent these are solid harmonics multiplied by a smooth radial function: the extra $h^2$ gives smooth Cartesian curvature, including the first harmonic. The v5 extra factor $h$ gave a direction-dependent second derivative at the source. Bearing dependence vanishes at the antipode. The method infers deposit shape from fallout evidence and supplies no eruption-specific winds or phase labels. [Coefficients and covariance](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/geometry.py)

There is no attenuation inferred from survey reach in v6. Power-law and exponentially tempered power-law means, broad directional terms and the quantitative likelihood determine continuation. The taper coefficient $s$ is inferred jointly with the other model weights; it does not depend on the sampled reach. Empty map areas and unsampled bearings supply no negative observations. The former reach operator remains available to replay v5 packets exactly. The complete Earth is the same integration domain in both versions. [Historical reach implementation](../../archive/2026-11-25-directional-v5/spread.py)

## Local geology and the meaning of a site reading

Two independent Matérn-5/2 processes represent spatial departures. The broad process has 0.80 of the covariance and length $\ell\sqrt{35^2+r^2}$ km. Its nonstationary determinant factor preserves positive definiteness and allows the dispersal geometry to evolve continuously. The local process has 0.20 of the covariance and a **fixed physical length** $\ell(35/3)$ km, retaining the preceding near-source scale without inflating it distally. Both use the equal-area chart; chart lengths can differ from geodesic lengths at great distance. Their common amplitude remains 1.15 natural-log units, and both processes taper by $b^2$ at the antipode. An extension that inferred a weaker amplitude was tested and rejected because it impaired geographic prediction and some measured-site fits. The study retains those results. [Nonstationary covariance construction, Paciorek and Schervish](https://doi.org/10.1002/env.785)

The broad process is fully coherent. For the local process, three nearby distinct locations can define a regional average only when their weakest normalized covariance is at least 0.20. Independent averages use at most one third of the station count. Half the remaining orthogonal local-contrast variance enters the coherent deposit; the rest enters the observation distribution as unresolved site heterogeneity. Thus nearby observations can support a shared local feature, while an isolated reading need not dictate a distant lobe. These averages define prior basis functions, **not additional observations**. Each source row contributes its original likelihood once. Broad and local bases are factored separately, with a remaining-variance tolerance of $10^{-5}$ and no fixed station-count cap. Geometry and both sets of modes are rebuilt using training locations only after every deletion.

This is a finite-basis field: all retained broad modes are coherent, but it is not an exact continuum Gaussian process away from its training centres. Unrepresented marginal spatial variance enters the observation distribution. The coherent field outside the survey is determined by the retained covariance columns and broad trend priors; it does not add an independent unseen fine-scale random surface. Conditional uncertainty and complete-volume intervals therefore depend on this representation as well as on the selected mean family.

The coherent fraction remains a common modelling assumption. Zero and one are structural sensitivities, not alternate inputs. The model cannot identify erosion, redeposition, sampling error or genuine local fallout variation from an isolated residual. All supported local thin and thick regions remain eligible; a simpler-looking contour is not by itself evidence for deleting a feature. [Current sensitivity and evidence review](../../comparisons/directional/geographic-regularity/README.md)

A measured thickness and a lower-only thickness both contribute a Gaussian log-thickness density at the stated value. An upper limit contributes a cumulative probability and a range contributes the probability between its two endpoints. Field uncertainty, residual site variance and reported measurement error enter the predictive distribution separately. Only an audited quantitative limit enters the fit for a non-detection. Qualitative observations have predicted thicknesses and, where a response was specified, a separate probability calculation. The same site is counted once. Proximal deposits can be eroded or redistributed and a distal marine core can record locally unusual thickness. The model cannot identify a cause from an isolated residual. [Observation contract](sites-without-measured-thickness.md) · [Uncertainty in tephra-thickness mapping, Engwell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00445-015-0942-y)

## Inference and checks

There are 864 equally weighted combinations before fitting. The radial scale $L$ is 8, 32, 128 or 512 km, the thinning exponent $p$ is 2.75, 4.5 or 7 and the exponential tempering $s$ is 0, 0.20 or 0.85. There are two harmonic structures, three local length multipliers (0.20, 0.50, 1.10) and four residual standard deviations (0.26, 0.48, 0.80, 1.20). These are explicit common modelling choices. All 864 log evidences and weights, including boundary fits, are saved for each partition. All geometry, priors that depend on station locations, coefficients and weights are rebuilt after deleting a region. The posterior weights condition on the specified candidate family. [Fitting algorithm](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/fit.py)

Conditioning on exact readings is analytic, using the smaller of coefficient and observation space. For bounds, expectation propagation operates only in the latent bounded-site subspace. Coincident bounds remain separate likelihood factors; unconstrained coefficient directions do not enlarge the solve. Two independently scrambled Sobol rules correct the proposal using the **original upper-limit or interval probabilities**. No bound is imputed as a measured thickness. Analytic Gaussian conditional moments and CDFs act as control variates, reducing unnecessary Monte Carlo variation in the completed field.

Screening uses two 64-point rules and retains 99.999% of estimated mass for refinement. Screening stores evidence summaries, then recomputes retained states; it does not retain hundreds of unnecessary dense covariance matrices. The final mixture retains at least 99.95% of estimated mass. Adaptive positive Gaussian components approximate corrected bounded-event distributions. Their CDFs are checked at fitted sites, the vent and twelve bearings at ten radii from 4 to 12,000 km, using five quantiles. The two reference CDFs include **independently corrected model weights**, so the convergence check detects uncertainty in model averaging as well as within-model integration. Checks require maximum pooled CDF error below 0.035, maximum scramble difference below 0.06, weighted relative effective sample size above 0.14, weighted evidence discrepancy below 0.10, proposal residual below $10^{-4}$, and positive corrected covariance. Failed checks trigger higher sample counts and additional Gaussian components; every attempted resolution remains in the reports. [Inference](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/conditioning.py) · [Compression](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/compression.py)

Where economical, posterior packets store only the constrained coefficient subspace and restore the orthogonal complement as a full standard-normal prior. A reconstruction check bounds coefficient mean and covariance error by $2\times10^{-10}$; otherwise the full factor is retained. This saves storage without deleting uncertainty. Versioned packets retain the v5 source gate, covariance and survey attenuation when replaying historical results. [Posterior representation](../../src/empirical_isopach/models/directional/multiscale/posterior.py)

Independent spatial integrations and mesh refinement check the median and both pointwise quartile maps. A separate calculation samples whole coherent deposits and integrates each draw over Earth. Total-volume 95% limits must agree between two spatial scrambles within 4%, between independent parameter populations within 10%, and after refinement within 8%, with a ceiling of 32,768 draws per population and 65,536 spatial points per scramble. After an initial spatial refinement, spatial resolution increases whenever the two rules differ by at least 1%; parameter populations double independently. Every larger population rechecks both spatial rules. This allocates effort to whichever uncertainty remains unresolved without relaxing any acceptance threshold. Historical v5 integration retains its original fixed refinement sequence. The archive retains all attempted resolutions, sector allocations and fractions inside the connected survey or beyond observed radii. The integral of the pointwise median can differ from the median integrated draw. Likewise the integrated pointwise quartiles and the 95% interval of total draws answer different questions. The support screen checks observed brackets, unsupported holes and deletion stability independently. The v6 display evaluates the actual posterior median at Cartesian display nodes, avoiding interpolation artifacts from a coarser whole-Earth integration mesh. This does not smooth or alter the fitted field. Each case includes a three-scale probability map for 1 cm and half its thickest displayed doubling level, with a 2 cm floor for the latter. These probabilities refer to the latent deposit. The observed-reading variance enters when predicting a site's recorded measurement or limit. ## Reproduction

The ordinary intake runs the multiscale fit and its historical directional controls. For the directional study alone, from the repository root, use a fresh work folder and the following commands. Use the same work and release folders for the validation and plots. [New eruption intake](../NEW_ERUPTION.md)

```sh
python -m benchmarks.directional_multiscale --work results/direction/fits --jobs 3
python -m benchmarks.directional_release --work results/direction/fits --output results/direction/maps
python -m benchmarks.directional_evaluation --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study --baseline archive/2026-11-25-directional-v5/current-cases
python -m benchmarks.directional_scale_maps --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study --baseline archive/2026-11-25-directional-v5/current-cases
python -m benchmarks.directional_morphology --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study/geometry.csv.gz --baseline archive/2026-11-25-directional-v5/current-cases
python -m benchmarks.directional_features --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study/secondary-features.csv.gz --baseline archive/2026-11-25-directional-v5/current-cases
python -m benchmarks.directional_predictive --work results/direction/fits --output results/direction/study/fold-marginals
python -m benchmarks.directional_resolution --release results/direction/maps --output results/direction/study/resolution-sensitivity.csv
python -m benchmarks.directional_challenges --output results/direction/challenges --jobs 3
python -m benchmarks.directional_challenges --output results/direction/previous-challenges --previous --jobs 3
python -m benchmarks.directional_experiments --challenges results/direction/challenges --baseline results/direction/previous-challenges --output results/direction/study
python -m benchmarks.regional --output results/direction/regional
python -m benchmarks.verify_directional_geography
python -m benchmarks.verify_directional_multiscale
```
