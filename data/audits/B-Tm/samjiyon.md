# The 7 m Samjiyon pumice record

**The original 1993 Pyongyang edition of *Geology of Korea* records a 7 m pumice bed at Samjiyon (삼지연, 三池渊).** A separate August 2002 field visit documented more than 1 m of gray-white pumice in the same general area. The 7 m observation deserves a place in the evidence ledger. Its location and its conversion to combined primary fallout from the 946 CE eruption require assumptions. A 1987 DPRK survey describes 1.6 m around Samjiyon and 7 m on a separately named plateau. The principal fit leaves the site out. Four refits show what follows from accepting different interpretations. [Source ledger](additional-evidence.csv) · [Conditional model results](../../../eruptions/b-tm/results/source-audit/README.md)

The monograph’s p. 26 considers the possibility that upper and lower Korean pumices represented different eruptions. It associates pollen evidence with regional sections. It gives no unit-by-unit ages or thicknesses at the 7 m exposure. Its proposed historic date for the younger eruption predates the modern tree-ring and radiocarbon dating of the Millennium eruption to late 946 CE. These observations justify testing attribution while keeping the reported thickness intact. [Modern chronology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2016.12.024)

Figure 10 in the national report maps maximum lithic clast size. Figure 11 is captioned as a thickness map. Neither yielded a surveyed Samjiyon section with described bed contacts in the copy examined. On p. 66 the authors discuss a possible additional southeast thickness maximum in a plume interpretation. ## What enters the calculations

| Reading at the workbook position | Model input | Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| Principal | No numerical constraint | The published locality-scale thickness remains available for independent comparison. |
| Entire deposit is both phases’ primary fallout | 700 cm measurement | The workbook accurately locates the exposure and the whole thickness represents combined Millennium fallout. |
| A verified primary component of the eruption | 700 cm lower limit | The whole 7 m belongs to Millennium fall. Additional Millennium fall may be missing from the measured component. |
| A complete stack containing fall and other deposits | 700 cm upper limit | All Millennium fall at this position is present, its preserved thickness represents primary fall, and part of the 7 m may come from other deposits. Loss, erosion or an omitted phase can invalidate this limit. |
| Alternative published thickness near Samjiyon | 160 cm measurement | Hoang’s 1.6 m of pumice substrate represents combined primary Millennium fall at the alternate workbook position. The source supplies neither the exact exposure nor this attribution. |

All the DPRK locality summaries above are withheld from the principal 68-observation fit and from the [thinning-rate comparison](../../../eruptions/b-tm/results/source-audit/thinning/README.md). The four alternatives each add one expressly conditional row to that same principal input. They hold the other sites and ensemble weights fixed. The three 7 m cases change the likelihood of the same number. The 1.6 m case changes the source and thickness. No record is moved to a town centre and the 2002 regional lower limit is kept out of the point likelihood. A section with surveyed coordinates, bed contacts and phase identification would determine which interpretation the field evidence supports.
