# Millennium Eruption airfall, second field audit

The principal dataset represents the local bulk thickness of airborne material from both phases of the late-946 CE Millennium Eruption. The field evidence supports **150 unique quantitative localities**. The preceding release used 71. The new source table has **75 measurements, 34 reported or interpreted minima, 28 two-sided intervals and 13 upper bounds**. The fitting table uses each lower-only value as a measurement at every distance. It preserves both endpoints of each interval and all upper limits. Each interpreted archive bound enters the likelihood once. The [site ledger](sites.csv) contains 272 entries, including revisited exclusions, named outcrops, specimens and investigated non-detections. A source ID links to the original or additional source catalogue. The locator identifies the table, page, unit, station or core. A principal ID can link an alias to the accepted observation. Numeric fields on excluded rows can describe the size of a local lens, a regional observation or a preserved component. The `kind` field identifies actual input rows. ## Sun et al. and the eastern field sections

This edition assigns **all airfall beds that Sun et al. called post-Millennium to the Millennium Eruption**. Sun’s original unit labels and historical correlations are preserved. Pan et al. identify the upper Baguamiao trachytic tephra with the late eruptive stage. Yang et al. reconstruct an early and a later fallout package east of the caldera and explicitly question primary emplacement of two thick upper deposits. The full-stack interpretation is principal. The competing facies and preservation readings are recorded for future fits. Mud, reworked caps, welded spatter, valley flows and surges retain their own facies assignments. Distal ash interpreted by the field author as airborne co-ignimbrite fallout can contribute where the section supports it. A reported component minimum remains identifiable as such when fitted at its reported value. Sun’s original chronology remains available by unit and dated material. For example, the two bulk-organic samples between SMFD-1 and SMFD-2 returned 840 ± 30 and 580 ± 30 radiocarbon years BP. Charcoal in peat below the DYT stack returned 50 ± 30 BP. Yang and colleagues challenge the fit between those dates and the field stratigraphy. The adopted Millennium assignment is an interpretation of the sections. The separate documentary report of roughly 3 cm of ash in 1702 has no demonstrated tie to a measured outcrop. ## Coverage and unresolved support

Reconciliation includes all 76 Yang workbook rows and 83 Costa supplement rows, earlier field and supplementary exclusions, Miyamoto’s numbered stops and mapped candidates, the cited Sun and Pan sections, and 39 Tokyo Metropolitan University specimens. The two compiler crosswalks preserve the earlier transcriptions and decisions alongside the final site identifiers. Source-confirmed positions, town or mire anchors and approximate map registrations remain distinct. Some marine values still depend on Yang’s cited station compilation because an independently measured primary section was unavailable. [Yang crosswalk](yang-crosswalk.csv) · [Costa crosswalk](costa-crosswalk.csv) · [Specimen inventory](tmu-specimens.csv)

The Korean literature reports 8–9 m at Sinmusong, 2–3 m at Mupho, ranges across crests and valleys, and a typical 1–4 m at the sampled comendite exposures of Iacovino et al. Those observations have value at their published support. Position, preservation, correlation or individual exposure thickness is unresolved. Moving the typical range to each petrological sample or an entire valley range to a village centre would overstate the spatial evidence. [Spatial observations](unlocated-thicknesses.csv) · [Search and registration record](search.md)

The dataset registers grouped omission, weaker-transfer, coordinate and facies alternatives. Future models can test the response of the proximal maximum, northeastern fan and distal geometry to those decisions. Existing maps and volumes belong to the previous 71-locality input.
