# Source search and recovery

Field and archive searches were conducted on 26 November 2026 under the eruption aliases B-Tm, Baegdusan–Tomakomai, Millennium, Changbaishan, Paektu, Tianchi, 白頭山 and 长白山. Individual station codes, translated place names and numbered outcrops were followed into the studies below. The search included the project’s original workbook and deposited source files, the supplied full-length field book and Chinese primary papers, primary institutional catalogues and theses, journal supplements, core reports and archaeological excavations.

| Source or search | Tables and observations recovered | Remaining limits |
|---|---|---|
| Yang S1, Costa S1, original project CSV | All 76 and 83 compiler rows individually cross-referenced to accepted evidence or specific dispositions. Original entries and signed files retained. | A legacy coordinate and a number alone cannot uniquely identify a primary cut among the Yuanchi outcrops. |
| Miyamoto, 白頭山火山の噴火史 | Checked the full book, contents, chapter 2 localities and stratigraphic columns, text on Units B–H, chapter 6 station table and numbered logs and photos. Used written local contact thickness or GPS before reading only the missing field from a figure. | Some survey symbols outside the named transect lack printed station codes. Some overprint, component non-detections, potential duplicates and flow-dominated sections remain in the figure ledger. |
| Sun, Pan, Wang, Machida, Chen, Zhao, Zou | Checked tables for measured components and coordinates, full field descriptions, dated material, photographs, site maps and older/newer correlations. Re-read Sun Figs. 1–6 against Yang Figs. 1, 3 and 6, including every post-ME-labelled fall. | Some coordinates are kilometre-scale. Coarse ash at SMFD and local cut-to-cut variation at LFZ are explicit alternatives. |
| Pan’s doctoral dissertation, Chinese and Korean geological accounts | Printed p. 24 and Fig. 3.7 in the dissertation distinguish the two southern exposures. Original legacy provenance identifies the English 1993 and Chinese accounts and their chapter/page numbers. Retained ranges as sector or locality observations. | The photograph is not keyed to a unique surveyed position. Several DPRK pumice descriptions cover whole settlements, landforms or catchments and lack contemporaneous fall contacts. |
| Nakanishi, Kase, JMA, GSJ, Tashiro, archaeological records | Surveyed all B-Tm cells and core counts in Nakanishi Table 2. Checked named geoslicer strata, radiocarbon-location table and other event beds. Read the station log at JMA-V105, the Kokutaiji A-3 lens and the depth-resolved Tashiro core. Honkayabe 1 provides a local range. Chikano records lumps in fills; Rokkasho’s footnote refers to surrounding exposures. | Unsampled directions, blank B-Tm cells and samples without documented recovery do not constrain a total-fall ceiling. |
| Tokyo Metropolitan University sample catalogue | All 39 B-Tm specimens reconciled by printed locality. Followed Tashiro, Lake Ogawara, Rokkasho and archaeological names into available field descriptions. | Some specimens only say Japan Sea, border area or North Korea. Specimen size and count do not provide a local bulk thickness. |
| Peat and marine archives | Checked original peer-reviewed peat papers, Zhang 2026 Fig. 2 and publisher supplements, Mendeley V1 file contents, Nakanishi publications, Derkachev's three-core scope and Novoselova’s station coordinates. | Publisher S2/S3 characterize material above the Millennium bed. Files S2–S4 in the separate Mendeley package identify Zhibian samples. Unscaled overview transfers disagree with independent local readings. |
| Primorye field transects and palaeolakes | Matched dated sections in Razjigaeva’s Table 2 to two Sergeev bogs, Muta, Shkotovskoe, Kit, Muravyinaya and Triozerye. Examined peat structure and glass redistribution. The 1993 coastal-glass survey identifies four possible Millennium occurrences without an event-specific loading calibration. Shufan core 12716, the Yuzhno-Ussuriysk fortress and the Boisman survey provide additional cryptotephra occurrences. | Some station tables anchor a mire or bay. Boisman’s printed coordinates conflict with its named coast. A 5 cm sample width or the number of analyzed shards alone cannot set a site-specific fallout bound. |
| Legacy cryptotephra and exclusions | Reconciled the prior ledger and additional-evidence IDs, archive response calibrations, upper limits, size-fraction budgets and sedimentary/non-primary alternatives. | Glass in a sample or the thickness of host sediment alone does not determine original total fallout. |

Pixel coordinates, calibration anchors, source checksums, mark-to-site assignments and individual close-up panels accompany the readings. Source/graphic reading intervals express uncertainty in reading the field evidence. They are separate from any future model’s prediction intervals.
