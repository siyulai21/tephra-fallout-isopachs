# Source audits and selection decisions

[Data guide](../README.md) · [Accepted CSVs](../eruptions/README.md) · [Reports](../../eruptions/README.md)

When a survey appears to publish its site data only on a map, document the search for the original tables before extracting values or coordinates. The audit protocol covers registration, bounds, duplicate surveys, numbered maps, visual review and reproducible fitting decisions. [Map digitization audit](../../docs/methods/map-digitization.md)

- The Mazama principal input includes 300 localities and 29 fitted intervals. The audit identifies the disposition of all 71 Buckland primary records and links each reported range to its field evidence. [Mazama principal audit](Mazama/principal-ranges/README.md)
- [Pinatubo 1991, Layer C](Pinatubo-1991/reaudit/README.md) links 235 accepted sites and 151 marine archive queries to field descriptions, source plates and original station records.
- [Sakurajima Satsuma (P14)](Sakurajima-Satsuma/README.md) traces 57 accepted observations to field descriptions, native workbooks and a marine core, with measurement coverage by distance and bearing.
- [Ilopango total TBJ airfall](Ilopango-TBJ/field-logs/README.md) reconciles the two Steen-McIntyre field reports, the modern unit workbook and the mapped fallout records at 168 fitted localities.
- [Ilopango TBJ Unit G, earlier edition](Ilopango-TBJ/reaudit/README.md) reconciles 116 accepted localities with the original workbook, field survey maps, lake and marine cores, and excavated sections.
- [B-Tm](B-Tm/README.md) derives three site-specific cryptotephra limits and tests alternative archive responses. Its earlier review covers 84 previously fitted records, 83 Costa Table S1 entries and additional primary field sites.
- [Minoan](Minoan/README.md): the complete published table, combined-fallout arithmetic, incomplete-core bounds and conflicting-row sensitivities.
- [YTT](YTT/README.md): all 88 reviewed source records, the principal 46-record selection, separate sensitivity inputs and [selection history](YTT/history/README.md).
- [Aso-4](Aso-4/README.md): Table S2 lineage, two cruise-coordinate corrections, source-qualified bounds, unresolved primary-source conflicts and marine transport limitations.

The original eruption CSVs retain supplied flags. Aso-4 and B-Tm now have separately named reviewed inputs. Mazama uses its source-qualified selection; Tambora retains its supplied flags. An audit records evidence and unresolved qualifications. It does not imply that modeling removes preservation or measurement uncertainty.

Source locations reflect their acquisition history: the supplied YTT workbook remains with its audit, while the downloaded Minoan workbook is under [sources/](../sources/README.md). Accepted model inputs are listed separately in [eruptions/](../eruptions/README.md).
