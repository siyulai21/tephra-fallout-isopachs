# Aso-4 at IODP Site U1429 · 25 November 2026

The principal Aso-4 input includes **27 cm** of recovered vitric tephra in Hole U1429B, core 6H, section 3. Row 95 is a point measurement in centimetres. Hole B lies at **31.61744833° N, 128.99752000° E**, in 731.64 m of water, about 243 km southwest of the project's Aso vent. The coordinates come from the shipboard Hole B entry, 31°37.0469′ N and 128°59.8512′ E. ## Bed thickness and identity

1. Sagawa et al. identify sample **NK224** as Aso-4 at **48.26 m CCSF-D**. Their age model adopts the eruption age of about 87 ka as a tie point. [Sagawa et al. (2018), Tables 1–2 and Figure 6](https://progearthplanetsci.springeropen.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s40645-018-0168-7.pdf)
2. The shipboard splice uses U1429B-6H from 46.52 to 49.99 m CCSF-D. The +3.100 m offset for that core places NK224 at 45.16 m CSF-A, approximately 126 cm into section 3. The matching bed spans 48.00–48.27 m CCSF-D and contains NK224 near its base. [Core offsets](https://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/346/109/109_t25.htm) · [Splice intervals](https://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/346/109/109_t26.htm)
3. The original shipboard workbook, `Tephra layer!A4:J4`, records the contacts at 100 and 127 cm in section 3, equivalent to 44.90 and 45.17 m CSF-A. Their difference is **27 cm**. The workbook records a bioturbated contact. Its core summary and the disturbance table place severe coring disturbance deeper in the core, beginning in section 5. [Original Hole B workbook](346_description_U1429B.XLSX) · [IODP visual core description](https://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/346/EXP_REPT/CORES/CORU1429.PDF#page=27) · [Disturbance intervals](https://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/346/109/109_t19.htm)

The Aso-4 correlation has high confidence. Primary marine fallout is the working interpretation for this discrete bed in bioturbated ooze. Marine focusing, redistribution and compaction remain possible. The measured recovered thickness enters the fit as a point, with no numerical correction for those processes. An omission fit can test how much the southwestern contour depends on this core. [Shipboard lithostratigraphy](https://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/346/109/109_3.htm)
