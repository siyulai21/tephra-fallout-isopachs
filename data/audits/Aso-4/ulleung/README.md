# Aso-4 in four Ulleung Basin cores

Chun, Ikehara and Han (2004) report a named Aso-4 tephra layer at four R/V Onnuri stations. Table 1 gives a pair of depths for each layer under “stratigraphic position.” We interpret the difference as the preserved layer thickness. Figure 2 distinguishes the tephra lithofacies from the surrounding mud, and Table 2 gives separate depths for glass samples. Only 95PC-6 has a published Aso-4 core photograph in this paper. Each principal input row records how its point estimate was derived. The authors tabulate Aso-3 separately. Their 87 or 89 ka ages for Aso-4 come from previous eruption dating. The authors identify the Aso tephras as airborne co-ignimbrite ash. They distinguish Aso-4 from the older Aso-3 using stratigraphy, shard form, glass chemistry and grain size. The photograph shows an irregular band in 95PC-6. For the other three cores, the logs support a layer interpretation but leave the sharpness of its upper and lower contacts unresolved. All four lack a published ash proportion or a correction for mixing, focusing, winnowing or compaction. Three sites lie near the Ulleung Deep-Sea Channel and 96EBP-7 lies on the flat basin floor. The latter has the weakest independent support for the Aso-4 attribution. Chun (2022) reproduces the same 95PC-6 photograph.
