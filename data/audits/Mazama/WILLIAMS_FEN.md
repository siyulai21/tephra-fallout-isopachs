# Williams Lake Fen and the two ash horizons

The principal dataset omits the 46 cm at B2020-113. The original S1 comment identifies two layers. Mehringer’s account records 9 cm of lake sediment separating the two horizons in this flood-formed basin between Badger and Williams lakes. The available field description does not resolve how much of the compiled 46 cm belongs to a single climactic airfall. [Investigator account in Johnson et al., p. 6](https://www.fs.usda.gov/pnw/pubs/pnw_gtr322.pdf) · [Buckland transcription](buckland2020-293-localities.csv)

The former 244-position input included the 46 cm. The current `restore-williams` comparison refits that decision on its own, while `prior-244` restores the entire former selection. The locality and its coordinates remain available for model predictions in both cases.
