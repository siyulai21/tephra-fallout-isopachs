## Original bulk fallout and preserved beds

The target includes the primary climactic fallout, including co-ignimbrite ash. Incomplete primary beds can give a minimum when recognizable ash was deposited in place. A stratigraphic bracket or mixed package can supply a ceiling conditional on containment of the original fall, sufficient capture and negligible thinning. The ledger retains depths, measurements, interpretations, coordinates, dating material and eruption assignments separately.

## Counts and the size of the recovered component

The original counts specify which component reached the sampler. For a selected set of nonoverlapping bins of width Δz, the volume-based inventory is the sum of concentration times Δz. Counts per gram multiplied only by Δz retain their original wet or dry mass basis. A complete dry mass balance must establish the horizontal area, retained target fraction and original bulk density. A proposed original thickness t requires effective response k ≤ J⁺/t for an upper reading J⁺. Each numerical profile includes response requirements for 0.5, 1, 5 and 10 cm.

Window totals and narrower-window alternatives measure recovered material. No independent local original bulk thickness and compatible efficiency bound were found for these samples. |

The assumptions differ by archive and are retained beside each result. The B-Tm case's published calibration uses different material, size recovery, units and sediment conditions. No independent common response floor was established for that transfer.
