## Duplicate and other-eruption controls

Swiftcurrent's48 cm mixed layer was already catalogue row150. The primary report confirms70–80% glass plus carbonate/clastic sediment, but those proportions do not establish a clean fallout thickness. The later Grinnell/Swiftcurrent paper repeats that horizon; it is not an additional likelihood row. Paired Yellowstone cores likewise remain individually listed while entering the fit once per shared location.

The [Wiejaczka–Giachetti2022 archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5874666) was downloaded and hashed in full. Its original workbook supplies26 numeric pit locations and thicknesses of **earlier Cleetwood phases**, not26 climactic Mazama thicknesses. All26 are retained as controls with the unchanged workbook. The article's12–232 cm climactic-overburden range lacks a site-by-site mapping and is not assigned to each pit. The article's28 pits versus26 numeric workbook rows remains unresolved. The separate2024 Cltwd2 measurement combines lower and upper pumice; current phase reinterpretation prevents calling the entire340 cm a secure upper-pumice minimum.

Marine records are retained separately by cruise/core ID. TT063-13 Mazama must not be confused with its Glacier Peak horizon, Y72-11-1P Wono, or F8-90-G21 Mono Craters glass. Additional Cascadia core leads include presumed isolated-basin airfall and turbidite-associated glass; no unmeasured primary thickness is inferred from core length or shard concentration. Mixed-soil magnetite records at Mosquito Creek and Mount Edith Cavell retain provenance uncertainty.

## Search boundary and unresolved work

This follow-up covered the three user-supplied papers, cited core/cruise reports, original repositories and deposited data; related Nevada, Yellowstone, Cascades, British Columbia and marine studies; and cited newer tephra, cryptotephra, and lake-history reports. Searches used Mazama with site names, core IDs, ash/tephra, thickness, mm/cm, trace, cryptotephra, reworked, primary, lake, marine, peat and thesis terms. Sources were searched for tables and field metadata before considering figures. No new position or thickness was digitized from a map in this follow-up.

The [primary-source work queue](primary-source-work-queue.csv) still contains257 original catalogue records with compilation-only verification, including87 personal-communication records. A queue item is not necessarily an inaccessible source: some have not received an independent primary check. Additional access gaps include the Long2014 full site details, Mark thesis original payload, Wershow field workbook/core positions, Steinman supplements/full2019 lithology, Thompson1984 Upper Dollar log, and original Wolf Meadow sampling records. Accessible indexed primary passages are labeled as such; they are not claimed to be downloaded full originals. The [access record](access-gaps.csv) and ledger retain these distinctions.

An exhaustive negative claim that no other Mazama occurrence exists is unsupported. This release provides a reproducible search and decision boundary, including numeric records still awaiting positioning or transport constraints. The source refits quantify the effect of accepted assumptions; they cannot certify unresolved identities or close the remaining literature gaps.
