# Dated sections and additional primary core logs

This source review recovered six quantitative field records, including two lake beds at corrected locations. The current audit records the preservation assumptions and fitted reading for each locality. The source checks below cover several other entries that contribute no additional likelihood. ## Checks on original quantities

- At Tumalo Lake the earlier Long, Power and Bartlein core log gives 50 cm from 4.84 to 5.34 m. It distinguishes this bed from two younger South Sister tephras. At Round Lake, McKay describes 14 cm at 267–281 cm, below the Sand Mountain and other mafic units. The registered 50 and 14 cm values already used those two lakes. McKay's rounded lake coordinates provide an additional location check for Tumalo where the earlier article's coordinate string conflicts. [Long et al., stratigraphy and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yqres.2010.08.010) · [McKay 2012, Table 3.2 and Fig. 3.8](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/bb66b5cf-7dc4-45fe-b393-a65af64635f6/content)
- Lost Trail Pass Bog has an 8 cm composite ash interval at 5.05–5.13 m and a separate 5 cm ash-rich gyttja immediately above it. Petrography and trace-element analysis identify the ash. The 7 cm separate account and its 4.9 cm seasonal primary component describe a different partition. The fit uses the 4.9 cm minimum. The adjacent radiocarbon samples are WSU-1552 and WSU-1553. The Montana primary-source land description anchors this site. [Mehringer et al. 1977, Tables 2–3 and Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.1080/00040851.1977.12003929)
- At Bonaparte Meadows the primary lab report places Tx-2882 immediately above the upper ash and Tx-2881 and Tx-2883 between two ash units. Its reported depths and dates corroborate the sequence. The paper gives no independent measurement for the compiler's 12 cm climactic bed. Simpsons Flats core 7 in Washington has Mazama at 680 cm depth. A depositional discontinuity between the bracketing samples covers the eruption, so their 78 cm outer interval cannot cap original local fallout. This mire is separate from Simpson Place Bog in Oregon. At Mud Lake the laboratory series refers to two cores with samples below the ash. It corrects the coring locality to the Conconully area. [Valastro et al. 1980, pp. 1096–1099](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033822200011590)
- At Harper Lake near Chase, 45 and 70 cm are depths to two volcanic ash layers. The original laboratory record gives 82–86 cm for the paired marl and plant samples and leaves the individual ashes unidentified. The primary description, official lake centre and printed coordinate disagreement are retained. [Lowdon and Blake, GSC Paper 73-7, Chase series](https://emrlibrary.gov.yk.ca/gsc/papers/73-7.pdf) · [Canadian Geographical Names Database, Harper Lake](https://geonames.nrcan.gc.ca/search-place-names/unique?id=JANPE)

## Laboratory ages and location conflicts

Egan’s Tables 6.1 and 12.2 provide 81 radiocarbon entries and two ice-core ages. The crosswalk reports the laboratory IDs, original references, both printed uncertainties, calibrated result as given, relation to the ash, material, published coordinates, filtered-model flag and candidate ledger IDs. It also gives distances to the current ledger locations using the table's reported coordinates. Thirteen radiocarbon entries have different printed uncertainty widths between the two tables. No recalibration or conversion between ice years and radiocarbon years was applied. [Age crosswalk](egan-age-crosswalk.csv) · [Original thesis, pp. 167–174 and 411–426](https://pure.manchester.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/54585340/FULL_TEXT.PDF) · [Extraction with source checksum](extract_egan_dates.py)

The 83 entries link to 48 candidate compilation IDs. Some identify a district, two possible shore locations, associated flow charcoal or duplicated laboratory material. Hager's table coordinates duplicate those of Deep Creek. Wildcat's tabulated coordinate is far from the Hooper lake, and several Muir Creek positions disagree with the original reports. A stratigraphic age is recorded even where direct measurement of primary fallout is still pending. Sample spacing enters a thickness calculation only where the primary investigator locates the ash within the same section and the necessary preservation assumptions are stated.
