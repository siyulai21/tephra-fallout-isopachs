# Four investigator archives and a separate Lofty Lake campaign

| Locality | What the original archive contains | Treatment in the regional fallout dataset |
|---|---|---|
| **Davis Lake, Washington** | The 1978 central Livingstone core A is registered to Cathy Whitlock (Barnosky). Eric Grimm’s 1995 chronology 296, control 1791, centres the Mazama marker at **724 cm** and records its **6 cm thickness**, matching Buckland’s endpoints at 721 and 727 cm. The working 6,850 radiocarbon yr BP is an assigned tephra age. Nearby core B supplied the dated material. | The curated core dataset corroborates the **6 cm preserved horizon** recorded in S1. Both refer to the same bed. The fitted value remains **≤6 cm** conditional on local retention. The compiled lake position and the archive’s site-level coordinates differ by about 5 km. The archive records the centre of the lake and supplies no core GPS. [Original dataset 550](https://api.neotomadb.org/v2.0/data/downloads/550) · [Barnosky (1981)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0033-5894(81)90046-6) |
| **Lofty Lake, Alberta, north bay** | Lichti-Federovich’s investigator archive identifies the Mott–Terasmae north-bay collection. Two chronologies give thickness **0.1 cm** at **398 cm** but both accompanying notes place Mazama at **598 cm**. S1 also lists 1 mm at 398 cm. Grimm’s 2005 chronologies share a 555 cm basal control and place 398 cm between dated horizons at 294 and 440 cm. | The record supports the presence of a thin marker. The depth conflict and the origin of the database thickness field prevent assigning a new original-fallout measurement. The existing conditional **≤0.2 cm** is Young’s published bulk equivalent, a separate estimate. [Pollen dataset 1656](https://api.neotomadb.org/v2.0/data/downloads/1656) · [Linked geochronology 8345](https://api.neotomadb.org/v2.0/data/downloads/8345) · [Lichti-Federovich (1970)](https://doi.org/10.1139/e70-089) |
| **Lofty Lake, March 1979** | Schweger and Hickman’s 294 cm core contains a **0.5 mm** tephra at **135 cm**. Sediment at 136–143 cm yielded **6,290 ± 100 conventional radiocarbon yr BP**. Blake reports the layer without an eruption name. | Separate evidence row **G1984-LOFTY79**. The date below the ash is younger than the expected climactic Mazama age on the same conventional scale. The registration, age and eruption identity do not justify counting the 0.5 mm as another Mazama thickness. [GSC Paper 84-7, p. 12, samples GSC-2876 and GSC-2885](https://doi.org/10.4095/120004) |
| **Wildcat Lake, Washington** | The S1 point falls inside the polygon for Mehringer’s 1972 core with Davis and Kolva. The archive has 133 pollen samples. The original WSU laboratory report identifies Mazama beneath sample WSU-1452 S, dated to 5,380 ± 130 conventional radiocarbon yr BP at 11.25–11.34 m downcore. Mehringer distinguishes the original investigation from a later 27.39 m core in 5 m of water and explicitly locates the Mazama sequence in the lake. | The archived pollen data supply neither bed contact for an in-place primary fraction. The **146 cm mixed accumulation** and conditional **≥1 cm component** retain the qualifications of their individual sources. Recovery of Blinman’s original profile is outstanding. [Wildcat series, WSU measurements II, p. 146](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033822200002435) · [Core dataset 15893](https://api.neotomadb.org/v2.0/data/downloads/15893) · [Mehringer’s field account](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r6/icbemp/science/mehringe.pdf) |
| **Wildhorse Lake, Oregon** | The S1 point falls within Mehringer’s site polygon. The investigator archive contains 82 pollen samples. The original field synthesis places this Steens Mountain lake and Fish Lake at different elevations and compares their pollen histories. | These records independently locate the Wildhorse collection. They do not establish the thickness of the in-place fraction of the mixed package. The compiled conditional **≥2 cm** stays flagged for original observer verification. [Core dataset 15894](https://api.neotomadb.org/v2.0/data/downloads/15894) · [Mehringer’s field account](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r6/icbemp/science/mehringe.pdf) |

The full audit retains source-specific dates, coordinates and verification grades. Adding the separate unnamed Lofty horizon brings the inventory to 867 records. The preceding 271-locality edition used 15 source scenarios. Its input and checksums are preserved at the published revision.
