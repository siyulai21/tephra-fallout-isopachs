# Paladio-Melosantos Figure 7 field review

The project curator reread 32 labels on the map of the 15 June climactic fallout. A second reader checked the original pixels, the field and compilation symbols, the named sections and the Koyaguchi sublayers. Fourteen previously unused sites entered the fit and two measured values changed. The other 16 entries remain in the evidence record. [Original plate](../../sources/pinatubo-1991/paladio-figure-7.gif) · [Published Figure 7 caption](https://pubs.usgs.gov/pinatubo/paladio/index.html) · [Full row ledger with before and after values and positions](paladio-figure-7-review.csv)

Filled field circles report Layer C. An explicit inequality bounds that thickness directly. An open circle reports the whole section, which may include layers A and B, so it supplies an upper bound on Layer C. Filled and open squares reproduce Koyaguchi and Listanco survey readings. Table 2 sections take priority within the published intake's 0.8 km overlap screen.
