# Calbuco 2015 external field evidence

Mingari and colleagues archived the April 2015 Calbuco field surveys for their deposit-reconstruction study. The geographic reach analysis uses the original observations from the release. The 2015 eruption did not supply an analogue thinning curve or a corridor parameter. The model settings were fixed before these files were evaluated. [Mingari et al. (2023)](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/16/3459/2023/) · [Zenodo data and licence](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7259531)

The two original observation files are `POST_DATA/grl54177.csv` (Van Eaton et al.) and `POST_DATA/reckziegel.csv` (Florencia Reckziegel). The original thickness bounds are in centimetres. The training survey contains 163 rows and the withheld survey contains 45. The audit preserves all 208 decisions. Four training zeros and one validation zero have no recorded site-specific detection limit. Two validation records, C11 and C22, give the same coordinates and 0.03 cm. One contributes to scoring. Published study counts can differ because of preprocessing. The vent coordinates, 72.618°W and 41.330°S, come from the release’s `calbuco.inp`. [Row-by-row audit](../../../comparisons/nested-reach/field-surveys/calbuco-2015/row-decisions.csv) · [Source preprocessing and held-survey comparison](../../../benchmarks/nested_reach_field.py)

The original distal isopach is labeled **0.1 mm**, equivalent to **0.01 cm**. We fitted the longitude and latitude axes to the printed graticule and recorded both crossings at ten half-degree parallels between 36.5° and 41°S. The comparison graphic adds magenta rings to the reproduced figure. Transect lengths use the WGS84 ellipsoid. A pixel represents about 0.0068° on these axes. Digitization precision and uncertainty in the original field interpretation are separate.
