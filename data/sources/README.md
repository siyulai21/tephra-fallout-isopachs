# Preserved published sources

[Data guide](../README.md) · [Audits](../audits/README.md)

- The Mazama collection identifies 163 acquired source files by checksum. It includes 58 public originals and gives source links and checksums for the other 105. The collection covers the Buckland supplements, Young’s field sections, primary lake and outcrop records, dating studies and distal cryptotephras. [Mazama source collection](mazama/README.md)
- The Pinatubo source collection pairs original Luzon maps with named marine beds, cruise positions and preservation evidence. [Original surveys and station tables](pinatubo-1991/README.md)
- The Satsuma records pair numerical locations with published field measurements and document which archaeological values were taken from drawings. [Source files and original reports](sakurajima-satsuma/README.md)
- The Ilopango sources include the field-stop table and appendices, modern unit workbook, mapped airfall readings and distal cores. [Original sources for total TBJ airfall and Unit G](ilopango-tbj/README.md)
- The B-Tm sources include the Yang compilation, Costa phase table, Hokkaido sections, Greenland particle series and Sihailongwan varve chronology. [B-Tm originals and attribution](b-tm/README.md)
- [Minoan published workbook and catalogue](minoan/README.md): source downloads, attribution and their distinct roles in the audit.
- The supplied YTT workbook remains in [the YTT audit](../audits/YTT/README.md), alongside its original provenance. It has not been moved or rewritten.

Source bytes and checksums are preserved. Derived selections live under `audits/`; accepted model CSVs live under `eruptions/`. A source file is not automatically an accepted training dataset. Follow its audit before using it.
