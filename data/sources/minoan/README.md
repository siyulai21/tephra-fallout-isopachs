# Minoan source material

Karstens, J. et al. (2023), **Revised Minoan eruption volume as benchmark for large volcanic eruptions**, Nature Communications 14, 2497. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38176-3), [open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10148807/).

- `supplementary_data_2.xlsx`: unchanged publisher Supplementary Data 2, `Tabelle1`, rows 3–107 (105 source records). [Original download](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-023-38176-3/MediaObjects/41467_2023_38176_MOESM5_ESM.xlsx).
- `pangaea_937486.tsv`: unchanged 2021 core catalogue used for cross-checking only, never appended as extra independent measurements. Kutterolf, S., Freundt, A., Druitt, T. H., McPhie, J., Nomikou, P., Pank, K., Schindlbeck-Belo, J., Hansteen, T. H. and Allen, S. R. (2021), *Thicknesses and occurrence of tephras in the Aegean and Mediterranean cores from POSEIDON cruise POS513 (Table 3)*, PANGAEA, [doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.937486](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.937486), part of [the bundled publication](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.937928).

Both sources are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). No changes were made to these source files. Prepared totals, lower bounds, exclusions and duplicate handling are our derived analysis and are documented in the [audit](../../audits/Minoan/README.md).

The paper's [Supplementary Information](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-023-38176-3/MediaObjects/41467_2023_38176_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) defines the fallout and turbidite units. The 2023 revised workbook takes precedence over older catalogue values; disagreements are retained as qualifications rather than silently overwritten. The older original field publications were not independently re-audited.
