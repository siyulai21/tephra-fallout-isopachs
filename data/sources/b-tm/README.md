## Microparticle and lake-stratigraphy data

Lee et al. (2024) provide the high-resolution NGRIP1 water-equivalent depth and insoluble particle series in Extended Data 3. The ice calculation uses the two mass-concentration columns in their original units. Figshare reports MD5 `0f32b97978d327c5f297b068f1d663ae` and the original workbook has SHA-256 `1f33754c1a813ad705045a61830c35429ecd39f595d9d8dc54c942a9be478347`. The dataset is licensed under CC BY 4.0. [Original workbook](lee2024-particles-and-geochemistry.xlsx) · [Versioned source](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26549344.v1)

Mingram, Stebich, Schettler and colleagues (2018) provide the independent varve chronology of Lake Sihailongwan. The original tab-separated file has SHA-256 `40c950eb6c0bd69ce20b43fa746c70163079c692f2d10347cea5c8715ab2de41`. This PANGAEA dataset is licensed under CC BY 4.0. [Original varve record](mingram2018-varves.tsv) · [Versioned source](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.885906)
