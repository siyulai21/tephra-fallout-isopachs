Ship positions come from the cruise logs wherever a published station number permits a match. ## Other 1991 deposits

The early phreatic ash, layer A of 12 June, preclimactic layer B and postclimactic layer D have distinct timing and footprints. These original maps and the stratigraphic chapter preserve that context. Adding thicknesses from different sampling locations would create unsupported totals. [Early fallout](context/paladio-figure-2.gif) · [Layer A](context/paladio-figure-3.gif) · [Layer B](context/paladio-figure-6.gif) · [Koyaguchi A and B](context/koyaguchi-figure-3ab.gif) · [Layer D](context/paladio-figure-13.gif)

Table 1 reports 0.014 km³ for layer A, 0.17 km³ for B and 0.20 km³ for D. For C the authors give 1.7 km³ with a single thinning segment, 3.0 km³ with two and 3.4–4.4 km³ with three, varying the assumed distal thickness. These are bulk-volume interpretations. The fitted observations come from individual sites. [Original Table 1](https://pubs.usgs.gov/pinatubo/paladio/index.html) · [All eight published Pinatubo scenarios](context/paladio-published-volume-comparisons.csv)
