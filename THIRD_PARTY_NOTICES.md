# Third-party map data

The atlas bundles `src/probabilistic_isopach/assets/ne_110m_land.geojson` from [Natural Earth's vector-data repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson).

Natural Earth data are public domain; see [the terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The 1:110-million land outlines provide context only, not precise coastlines or input to the fit. This attribution does not change ownership or licensing of the original code, measurements or historical figures.
